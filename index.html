Scope: One barista, one espresso station. Every used portafilter is cleaned once. A batch ends only after the last shot has been extracted and all its portafilters have been cleaned. Grinding and tamping occupy Barista 1 in this illustrative model. The machine can extract while the barista prepares the next portafilter. Times and overlaps are assumptions, not field measurements; verify against the actual deli workflow.
